// Current protocol snapshot

Training

  • Bodyweight calisthenics and HIIT, 4 days a week
  • One extra day for core only
  • Morning session, fasted
  • Water with a pinch of salt during training
  • Core metric: heart rate recovery, 156→119 bpm in 2 min
  • Resting heart rate around 50 bpm

Fasting 18:6

  • 18 hours fasted, 6-hour eating window, every day
  • Meal 1: protein, fats and vegetables, starch kept low
  • Meal 2: protein with complex carbs
  • Apple cider vinegar in water before each meal
  • No added sugar since age 20

Supplements

  • Creatine monohydrate, 5 g
  • Omega-3: EPA 2500 mg + DHA 875 mg
  • Ubiquinol CoQ10, 100 mg
  • Vitamin D3 2000 IU with K2
  • Magnesium glycinate 360 mg, one hour before sleep
  • Vitamin C 1000 mg and a probiotic with meal 2

This is my own protocol, shared as data. It is not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before you change yours.
